# Dropshipping Product Opportunities — Top 10 Validated Picks

> **Budget:** $2,000 | **Platform:** Shopify | **Market:** US | **Target Gross Margin:** 40%+ | **Break-even CAC:** 30% of AOV

---

## Evaluation Framework (5-Factor Scoring, max 25)

| Factor | What It Measures | Score 1 (Bad) | Score 5 (Excellent) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Demand Stability | Consistent sales vs. seasonal spikes | Purely seasonal/fad | Year-round consistent |
| Landed Cost Margin | Profit after ALL costs (COGS, shipping, fees, tariffs) | <15% | >40% |
| Return Rate Risk | Category-typical return rate | >20% | <5% |
| Supplier Density | Number of reliable suppliers available | Single-source | 5+ verified |
| Competition Density | Seller saturation on target channels | >500 sellers | <100, differentiation possible |

**Minimum viable score: 15/25.** All products below score ≥17.
