# Executive Summary — LED Light Therapy Mask Dropshipping Launch

> **Product:** Portable LED Light Therapy Mask (7-Color, Wireless, Rechargeable)
> **Budget:** $2,000 | **Platform:** Shopify | **Market:** US | **Test Period:** 14 days or 50 orders

---

## Opportunity Overview

The at-home LED skincare device market is projected to reach $1.8B globally by 2027 (CAGR 12.5%). Consumer demand for salon-quality skincare at home has accelerated post-pandemic, with "LED face mask" search volume up 25% YoY in the US. The product sits in a sweet spot: premium enough to command $55–$60 retail, inexpensive enough to source at $15–$18 landed, and lightweight enough for $3–$5 shipping.

## Five Key Risks & Mitigations

### Risk 1: Supplier Quality/Reliability Failure
- **Probability:** Medium (20%)
- **Impact:** High — delayed orders, refunds, negative reviews
- **Mitigation:** Order samples from 2+ suppliers before launch. Maintain a backup supplier. Use CJ Dropshipping (US warehouse) for initial orders to ensure 3–5 day delivery. Set 48-hour processing time SLA.

### Risk 2: Ad Account Suspension or Poor ROAS
- **Probability:** Medium (25%)
- **Impact:** High — halts customer acquisition
- **Mitigation:** Comply with all ad policies (no before/after claims without disclaimers). Diversify across TikTok + Meta from day 1. Maintain organic content pipeline as backup. Set automatic budget kill switch at $20 CPA (2× target).

### Risk 3: Market Saturation / Price War
- **Probability:** Low-Medium (15%)
- **Impact:** Medium — compressed margins
- **Mitigation:** Differentiate on branding, not price. Build email list from day 1 for retention marketing. Avoid competing below $49.99. Focus on value-added content (skincare routines, usage guides).

### Risk 4: Tariff/Import Cost Increase
- **Probability:** Medium (20%)
- **Impact:** Medium — 5–15% margin compression
- **Mitigation:** Use US-warehoused inventory via CJ Dropshipping (tariffs pre-paid in bulk). Monitor trade policy updates. Build 10% tariff buffer into pricing model.

### Risk 5: High Return Rate / Chargebacks
- **Probability:** Low (10%)
- **Impact:** Medium — margin erosion
- **Mitigation:** Detailed product descriptions with exact specifications. Video demonstrations on product page. Clear return policy (30-day, customer pays return shipping). Proactive customer support templates.

## Decision Criteria

|---|---|---|---|
| CPA (Cost Per Acquisition) | > $20 | $10 – $20 | < $10 |
| Conversion Rate | < 1.0% | 1.0 – 2.5% | > 2.5% |
| Return Rate | > 8% | 4 – 8% | < 4% |

| Customer Satisfaction (reviews) | < 3.5 stars | 3.5 – 4.2 stars | > 4.2 stars |
