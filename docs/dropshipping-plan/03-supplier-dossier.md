## Quality Checklist (Upon Sample Receipt)

- [ ] LED lights function on all 7 color modes
- [ ] Battery holds charge for 20+ minutes of use
- [ ] USB-C/micro-USB charging cable included and functional
- [ ] Silicone/material is comfortable, no chemical smell
- [ ] Elastic straps are adjustable and secure
- [ ] Packaging is clean, no damage, includes user manual
- [ ] Overall build quality: no loose wires, cracks, or sharp edges
- [ ] Light intensity is consistent across all LEDs
- [ ] On/off and mode buttons are responsive
- [ ] Compare both samples side-by-side for consistency

## Supplier Risk Contingency

| Risk | Trigger | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Primary supplier stock-out | <20 units in US warehouse | Switch to AliExpress backup (accept 12–18 day shipping) |
| Quality decline | 2+ customer complaints about defects in a week | Pause orders, inspect new sample, consider switching |
| Shipping delays | Average delivery > 10 days | Escalate with CJ support, evaluate air freight from Alibaba |
| Communication breakdown | No response in 48 hours | Switch to backup supplier immediately |
| Price increase > 10% | Supplier raises prices | Negotiate or switch; adjust retail price if needed |
