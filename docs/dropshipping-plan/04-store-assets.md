## Product Description (487 words)

### Transform Your Skin With Clinical-Grade LED Technology

Professional LED light therapy used to cost $200+ per session at a dermatologist's office. The GlowPro LED Face Mask brings that same technology home — for a fraction of the price.

### Why LED Light Therapy Works

LED (Light Emitting Diode) therapy uses specific wavelengths of light to penetrate skin at different depths, stimulating cellular processes that improve skin health. Each color targets different concerns:

- **Red Light (630nm):** Stimulates collagen production, reduces fine lines and wrinkles, promotes skin elasticity
- **Blue Light (470nm):** Targets acne-causing bacteria, reduces breakouts, calms inflammation
- **Green Light (520nm):** Evens skin tone, reduces hyperpigmentation, calms irritated skin
- **Yellow Light:** Reduces redness, soothes sensitive skin, improves lymphatic flow
- **Cyan Light:** Reduces swollen capillaries, promotes skin healing
- **Purple Light:** Combines red and blue benefits, anti-aging + anti-acne
- **White Light:** Penetrates deepest, accelerates tissue metabolism, tightens skin

### What Makes GlowPro Different

**Completely wireless.** No awkward cords or wall outlets. The built-in rechargeable battery powers 5+ full 30-minute sessions on a single USB-C charge. Use it on the couch, in bed, or while traveling.

**Comfortable fit for every face.** Adjustable elastic straps and flexible silicone construction conform to your unique face shape. The lightweight design (under 8 oz) means no neck strain during treatments.

**Real results, real savings.** A single professional LED facial costs $150–$300. The GlowPro pays for itself after just one use. Dermatologists recommend 3–5 sessions per week for optimal results — that's $450–$1,500/week at a spa, or $0 per session with GlowPro.

### How to Use

1. Cleanse your face and pat dry
2. Select your LED mode using the one-touch button
3. Secure the mask with the adjustable straps
4. Relax for 15–30 minutes
5. Remove and apply your favorite serum or moisturizer
6. Use 3–5 times per week for best results

Most users see visible improvement in skin texture and tone within 2–4 weeks of consistent use.
