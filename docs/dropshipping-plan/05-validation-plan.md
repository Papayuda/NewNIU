> **Goal:** Reach 50 orders OR complete 14 days of testing to determine product viability
> **Budget allocated:** $700 advertising + $117 Shopify + $93 samples = $910 validation budget

## Campaign Structure

### TikTok Ads

**Campaign objective:** Conversions (Purchase)

**Ad Group 1 — Broad Interest**
- Targeting: Women 22–45, US, interests: skincare, beauty, wellness, self-care
- Placement: TikTok feed
- Creative: Video 1 (unboxing) + Video 2 (in-use)
- Budget: 50% of TikTok allocation

**Ad Group 2 — Lookalike / Narrow**
- Targeting: Women 25–40, US, interests: LED therapy, anti-aging, dermatology, acne treatment
- Placement: TikTok feed
- Creative: Video 3 (before/after) + Static ad 1
- Budget: 50% of TikTok allocation

### Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Ads

**Campaign objective:** Conversions (Purchase)

**Ad Set 1 — Broad Skincare**
- Targeting: Women 25–55, US, interests: skincare routine, anti-aging, beauty devices, Sephora, Ulta

- Targeting: Website visitors (pixel), add-to-cart abandoners

## End-of-Test Decision Matrix (Day 20)

| Result | Action |
|---|---|
| **50+ orders, CPA < $15, ROAS > 3×** | SCALE — Increase ad budget to $100/day, begin Alibaba branding order |
| **30–50 orders, CPA $15–$20, ROAS 2–3×** | OPTIMIZE — Test new creatives, adjust targeting, run A/B on price |
| **15–30 orders, CPA $20–$25, ROAS 1.5–2×** | CONTINUE with caution — 7 more days of testing with new angles |
| **< 15 orders, CPA > $25, ROAS < 1.5×** | KILL — Pivot to Product #2 (Pet Self-Cleaning Brush) |
| **High returns (> 8%)** | PAUSE — Investigate quality issue, switch supplier, consider pivoting |
