## TikTok Ads Strategy

### Campaign Structure
```
Campaign: GlowPro — Conversions (Purchase)
├── Ad Group 1: Broad Skincare (Women 22-45)
│   ├── Ad: Unboxing video (15s)
│   ├── Ad: In-use demo (20s)
│   └── Ad: UGC-style testimonial (15s)
├── Ad Group 2: Anti-Aging Focus (Women 30-50)
│   ├── Ad: Before/after sequence (15s)
│   └── Ad: "Replaced my $200 facials" story (20s)
└── Ad Group 3: Acne Focus (Women 18-30)
    ├── Ad: Blue light demo + acne results (15s)
    └── Ad: Morning routine featuring mask (20s)
```

### TikTok Creative Best Practices
- Hook in first 2 seconds (text overlay + visual surprise)
- Native/organic feel — NOT polished commercial style
- Trending sounds when possible
- Vertical format (9:16)
- Refresh creatives every 5–7 days to avoid fatigue

## Micro-Influencer Strategy

### Budget: $150 (product seeding — no cash payments)

**Target Profile:**
- 5,000–50,000 followers
- Skincare, beauty, wellness niche
- US-based
- Engagement rate > 3%
- Has posted about skincare devices or routines before

**Outreach Message:**
```
Hi [Name]! 👋

I love your skincare content, especially your recent post about [specific post]. 
We just launched GlowPro — a wireless LED light therapy mask with 7 wavelengths 
for anti-aging, acne, and skin rejuvenation.

I'd love to send you one for free (no strings attached). If you love it, 
a quick review or story mention would mean the world to us. We'll also 
set you up with a unique discount code for your followers (they save 15%, 
you earn 10% commission on sales).

Would you be interested? I can ship it out this week!

Best,
[Your Name]
GlowPro Skincare
```

**Target: 10 outreach messages → 3–5 acceptances → 2–3 posted reviews**

**Expected impact:** 5,000–15,000 impressions per creator, 1–3 sales per creator, plus UGC content for paid ads.

## KPIs to Watch

### Daily Monitoring (check at 9 AM and 5 PM)
| KPI | Source | Alert Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| Ad spend vs. budget | TikTok/Meta dashboards | > 120% of daily target |
| CPA (Cost Per Acquisition) | TikTok/Meta dashboards | > $20 (pause ad set) |
| ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) | TikTok/Meta dashboards | < 1.5× (review creative) |
| CTR (Click-Through Rate) | TikTok/Meta dashboards | < 0.8% (refresh creative) |
| Cart abandonment rate | Shopify analytics | > 80% (check checkout flow) |

### Weekly Review (every Monday)
| KPI | Target | Action if Below |
|---|---|---|
| Blended ROAS | ≥ 2.5× | Reduce spend, test new creative |
| Email open rate | ≥ 30% | A/B test subject lines |
| Email click rate | ≥ 3% | Improve CTA placement |
| Customer acquisition cost | ≤ $15 | Optimize targeting |
| Site conversion rate | ≥ 1.5% | A/B test product page |
| Return rate | ≤ 5% | Investigate quality/expectation mismatch |

---

## Escalation Rules

| Trigger | Severity | Action |
|---|---|---|
| CPA > $25 for 3+ consecutive days | 🔴 Critical | Pause all ads. Review creative, targeting, landing page. |
| ROAS < 1.0× for 48 hours | 🔴 Critical | Pause campaign. Diagnose: creative fatigue? Wrong audience? |
| Ad account suspended | 🔴 Critical | File appeal immediately. Switch to backup channel. |
| Supplier stock-out | 🟡 Warning | Switch to backup supplier. Update shipping times on site. |
| Daily ad spend > 150% of budget | 🟡 Warning | Check for automated bidding issues. Apply spend cap. |
| CTR declining 3 days in a row | 🟢 Monitor | Prepare new creative. Test in 48 hours. |
| Cart abandonment > 85% | 🟡 Warning | Check for checkout bugs. Add trust badges. Test price. |
| Review < 3 stars received | 🟢 Monitor | Respond promptly. Investigate product quality. |
